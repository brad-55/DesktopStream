What is this?

Desktop Stream streams the screen and sound of a Windows PC to a phone's browser in real time. The phone only needs a browser; there is no app to install. You can also control the PC's mouse from the phone.

Video travels directly between the PC and the phone (P2P), so it is never stored on an outside server.

On the PC, Desktop Stream runs as a Windows service and starts listening automatically when the PC starts. You do not need to launch it each time. It can also show and control the UAC prompt and the lock screen.

About the room ID

The room ID is a 32-character passphrase used in Anywhere mode. When the PC and the phone have the same room ID, they find each other and connect.

Showing it

On the PC, right-click the tray icon and choose "Open console" (double-clicking the icon also works). The console shows the room ID, when it was created, and QR codes.

There are two QR codes side by side.

QR codeWhat it contains
Room ID (for remote access)The passphrase for Anywhere mode. Scan it on the phone
Connection URL (page to open)The URL of the page to open on the phone. Use the drop-down above it to choose the page for use from anywhere (the published page), or a Tailscale / LAN / localhost URL for Home mode

Which route works depends on your network, so the drop-down does not choose automatically. Pick the one you normally use (for example, Tailscale).

Regenerating it

Press "Regenerate room ID" in the console or the tray menu to get a new ID. Use it when someone else may have learned the ID. After regenerating, scan the QR code on the phone again.

Anyone who knows the room ID can see and control your PC. Treat it like a password.

Tips for quality and settings

Choosing a codec (compression method)

NameCharacteristics
QSV / NVENC / AMF / MFHardware encoding. Light on the PC and fast, so try these first. Intel: QSV or MF; NVIDIA: NVENC; AMD: AMF
H.264The most reliable. Choose this if unsure. Screen recording on the phone also works
H.265Better quality than H.264 with less data. However, some phones cannot show it, and screen recordings come out black
VP9Better quality than H.264, and screen recording works. A good choice if the phone supports it
x264 / x265Software encoding. Puts load on the PC's CPU (for comparison and testing)
AV1Experimental. Heavy, and often does not display

What to adjust when the video stutters

Try these in order: (1) lower bitrate (for example 1000 → 600), (2) lower mtu (for example 1000 → 800), (3) raise buffer (for example 300). Changes take effect when you press Start again.

When the video gradually falls behind (lag)

After a long session, the video can fall behind what is actually on the PC. Try the following, from the top.

MethodEffectSide effects
⚡ Cut lag buttonClears the accumulated delay on the spotThe video pauses for a moment. Restream viewers stay connected
Set the mode to "Real-time (low latency)"Delay builds up lessOn an unstable connection, the video may stutter a little more
Reconnect buttonThe most reliableThe video drops for a few seconds, and restream viewers are disconnected once

Choose the mode in the Settings tab. "Smooth playback" favors smoothness; "Real-time (low latency)" favors low delay. In real-time mode, the video catches up automatically when the delay grows.

When sound and video are out of sync

Press the 🔊 A/V sync button. It evens out how much video and audio are buffered on the receiving side, which removes the offset. If the stats show something like A/V: video +600ms, the offset is that large. In real-time mode, the offset is adjusted automatically when it grows.

The stats at the bottom of the screen show where the delay comes from (buf: = delay buffered on the receiving side, rtt: = network round-trip time, undec: = decoding delay).

Mouse and input

Use the "Input" drop-down to switch between two methods.

MethodBest forHow it works
🖱 TrackpadPhones and tabletsThe pointer moves as far as you slide (default)
⌨ Text inputTyping longer textType in the input box, then send

When you control from a computer, there is nothing to switch. If "Use a physical mouse and keyboard directly on the video" is enabled in Settings, moving the mouse over the video moves the PC's cursor to that spot. Touch is handled as the trackpad and the mouse as direct control, so you can use both at the same time.

In direct control, keys you type go straight to the PC without the input box, and Japanese input methods work as well. However, Ctrl+W, Alt+Tab and the Win key on its own are taken by the browser first, so they cannot be sent. Send them with "Send keys" or the dedicated buttons instead.

You can also control windows running as administrator, such as Task Manager, and the UAC prompt.

How the pointer moves

The PC's cursor moves along while you slide your finger. So menus that open when the pointer rests on them, and the resize arrows (↔ ↕) at the edge of a window, work as usual.

If someone at the PC moves the mouse, the pointer on the screen jumps to that position. While you are moving it from the phone, your input takes priority.

Scrolling applies to the window under the pointer. Place the pointer over the window you want to scroll.

Special keys

Keys that Windows treats specially cannot be sent with the normal key sending. There are dedicated buttons for them.

ButtonWhat it does
Ctrl+Alt+DelShows the security screen. Use it on the lock screen or sign-in screen
LockLocks the PC (same as Win+L)

Typing ctrl+alt+del or win+l in "Send keys" does the same as the buttons. If sending fails, the reason is shown in the log.

Restream

This feature shows the screen you are receiving on the phone to other people, with comments.

Starting a restream

Open the Restream tab, check the number of rooms (how many people can watch; the default is 10), and press "📡 Start restream". Send the URL or QR code that appears to the people you want to show it to. Your microphone is streamed too (switch with "🎙 Mic ON / 🔇 Mic muted").

What you can do

Switch the videoChoose remote desktop, camera (rear), or still image. You can switch while streaming, and changes to the still image's crop frame apply immediately
PC audio"PC audio ON/OFF" adds the PC's sound (such as a video's audio) to the stream. It is sent separately from the microphone, so viewers can balance the two volumes themselves
Mini previewA small window in the Restream tab shows what is actually being streamed
CommentsViewer comments appear in the Comments tab, newest first, and are shared with all viewers
Viewer count"Viewers: n/n" shows how many are watching now
Join and leave noticesViewers joining and leaving are shown in the Comments tab. A history per viewer (name, time, connection type) stays at the bottom of the Restream tab even after they leave

For viewers

Opening the URL you received puts you in a free room automatically. Choose a name and a color, and you can post comments. First-time viewers see a name screen, but their seat is already reserved while they type (the video starts after they enter a name). When the stream is full, no one else can join.

After you press the audio button to turn it to "🔊 Audio ON", the two sliders below the player adjust 🎬 Video (the PC's sound) and 🎙 Streamer mic separately (the mic starts a bit louder). The stream title is shown over the bottom of the video; tap the video to show or hide it. "⛶ Fullscreen" switches to full screen, and "✕ Exit fullscreen" at the top right returns.

The viewer page is in Japanese or English depending on the viewer's browser language.

More viewers means more load on the streaming phone and more data (about 500 kbps per viewer). Start with two or three people. If streaming breaks up while the phone is in the background, video and audio are reacquired automatically when you return to the page.

Handy features

Put it on the home screen (use it like an app)

Choose "Add to Home Screen" in the phone's browser to open it full screen from an icon. Without the address bar, you get more of the screen.

Recent rooms (Anywhere mode)

The "Enter room ID" screen lists up to 10 rooms you connected to recently, newest first. Press "Use" to switch to that room.

You can name each entry. A room ID is 32 random characters, so a name such as "Home PC" or "Work laptop" makes it easy to tell several PCs apart. Remove entries you no longer need with "×". Because room IDs are too long to retype, you are asked to confirm first.

The list shows room IDs with the middle omitted (aaaa1111…4444). A room ID is effectively a password, so the full value is not listed on screen.

Saved settings

Quality (bitrate), mode, Direct P2P, input method, pointer style, restream settings and so on are kept the next time you open the page. Settings are saved per device, so a phone and a computer can have different settings.

The codec and the screen (monitor) are restored only if the previous choice is still available. This prevents Start from failing with a setting that no longer exists, for example after connecting to a different PC or unplugging a monitor.

Automatic reconnection

Between pressing Start and pressing Stop, a dropped connection is reconnected automatically. This covers cases such as a weak signal or switching to another app on the phone and back. Reconnection attempts are logged with timestamps in the Connect tab.

To stop reconnecting, press Stop. Stop works even while a reconnection is in progress.

Staying connected while you use other apps

If you go to the home screen or open another app, the connection is kept for a while. However, this only works while the audio is "🔊 Audio ON". With no sound playing, the phone's OS sees no reason to keep the browser running.

Zooming in

Spread two fingers on the video to zoom from 1× to 4×. The spot you touch first becomes the center. While zoomed, moving the pointer away from the middle of the screen makes the view follow the pointer. While you work with fine movements near the middle, the view stays still. "Reset to 1×" at the top left returns to normal size.

The pointer arrow keeps its size when zoomed, so it is easy to aim.

Fullscreen and text input

In fullscreen, press "⌨" to show a text box below the video. The video shrinks slightly to make room. While the keyboard is shown, the tabs are hidden; they come back when you close the keyboard.

In the normal screen, when you switch to text input, only the video and the text box stay while the keyboard is shown, and the buttons between them (audio, fullscreen, volume and so on) are hidden for the moment. The keyboard takes up about half the screen, so with the buttons left in place the video and the text box could not both be seen. The buttons come back when you close the keyboard.

Troubleshooting

No video

  • Switch the codec to an H.264 type and press Start again (the most reliable)
  • In Anywhere mode, check that the room ID on the phone is the same as the PC's
  • If the phone's log shows pc failed, your combination of networks may not allow a direct connection. Changing location (network) may help

No sound

Press the audio button so it shows "🔊 Audio ON". Browsers require the page to start muted (OFF). If you are watching a restream and hear only one of the two sources, check that the "🎬 Video" and "🎙 Streamer mic" sliders below the player are not turned down.

Choppy video

Try the order in "Tips for quality and settings". If lost in the stats keeps rising, the quality is too high for the connection.

Start cannot be pressed, or it stays at "Authenticating…"

A device connecting for the first time cannot do anything until someone presses "Yes" in the approval dialog on the PC. Once approved, the device is registered and authenticated automatically from then on. Approval is required in Home mode (LAN) too.

When the approval dialog cannot be shown, for example on the sign-in screen, approve with a one-time PIN in the console on the PC instead. The console's "Devices awaiting approval (PIN)" lists the connecting device's name next to its PIN. If you recognize the device, enter that PIN and press "Allow this PIN".

Approval is required so that not just anyone who can join the same network can control the PC.

Some windows cannot be controlled

Normally, windows running as administrator and the UAC prompt can both be controlled. If they cannot, check that the service is running (right-click the tray icon and see whether it says "Status: running").

Ctrl+Alt+Del or Lock does not work

Reinstalling the service applies the required settings automatically. Run the install batch file again. If it still does not work, check the reason shown in the client's log after pressing the button.

The PC starts but does not begin listening

Check that "Auto-start" is on in the console or the tray menu.

If the console warns that an old startup entry remains, press "Remove". With the old entry left in place, a second DesktopStream starts separately from the service, and the two get in each other's way.

Someone may have learned the room ID

Use "Regenerate room ID" in the console or the tray menu to get a new ID, then scan it again on the phone. Also remove any devices you do not recognize from "Registered devices" in the console. A removed device is disconnected immediately, even if it is connected.

The video stops after a while with nothing happening

If the screen does not change at all for a while, screen capture on the PC may stop. This does not happen while a video or audio is playing. There is a mechanism that recovers automatically within a few seconds, but moving the mouse or pressing ⚡ Cut lag also brings it back.

The phone cannot connect

Check the following in order.

  • In the console on the PC, "Firewall" shows "Registered" (if not, a warning appears and you can add the rule with a button)
  • The browser is not showing a certificate warning. The first time, you need to allow it (in Chrome: "Advanced" → "Proceed")
  • In the console's Connection URL QR code, you chose the route you actually use (Tailscale, LAN, and so on) before scanning

Switches for troubleshooting (advanced)

Creating an empty file with a specific name in the following folder changes the behavior. Creating it requires administrator rights, and the service must be restarted afterwards.

C:\ProgramData\DesktopStream\

File nameEffect
capture_wgcChanges the screen capture method. For working around the video stopping when nothing changes on screen (you can also switch with the bundled set-capture-api.bat)
no_stall_watchTurns off automatic recovery when the video stops
no_tlsListens without encryption (http). For finding the cause when you cannot get past the certificate warning. Note that the microphone and camera stop working when the page is opened by IP address
worker_as_userRuns with the older, lower privileges. The UAC prompt and the lock screen will no longer be shown
lang_en / lang_jaFixes the language of the PC side (tray, approval dialog, console). You can also choose it in the console's "言語 / Language"

If you create the file with Notepad, it gets an extension such as no_tls.txt; it is still recognized.

Where the logs are

The logs are in the following folder and can be opened with Notepad. The logs are written in Japanese.

C:\ProgramData\DesktopStream\logs\ (service.log = the service, worker.log = the streaming process)
