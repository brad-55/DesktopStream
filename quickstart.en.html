🖥️ Desktop Stream Quick Start

Show your PC's screen on your phone in 5 minutes

日本語

Goal of this guide: view your Windows PC's desktop and hear its sound in your phone's browser. There are no difficult settings. Follow the steps from the top.

1Install it on the PC

Run the DesktopStream-…-setup.exe you downloaded. When Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?", choose "Yes".

When the installation finishes, DesktopStream is registered as a service and starts automatically. You are ready when its icon appears in the taskbar tray. From now on it starts listening whenever the PC starts.

Administrator permission is needed at this prompt (UAC) so that the phone can also control Task Manager and the UAC prompt itself.
For the portable version (…-portable.zip), right-click install-service.bat in the extracted folder and choose "Run as administrator".

2Show the room ID

Right-click the tray icon and choose "Open console". The console shows the room ID (letters and numbers) and QR codes.

The room ID is a passphrase. Anyone who knows it can connect to your screen, so do not show it to others.

3Open the client on your phone

Open this page in your phone's browser.

https://brad-55.github.io/DesktopStream/client.html

You can also open it by scanning the "Connection URL" QR code in the console.

On the same Wi-Fi, https://PC-IP-address:8080 also works (no room ID needed). In that case the browser shows a warning the first time because the certificate is self-signed; continue past it (in Chrome: "Advanced" → "Proceed").
The page appears in English when the phone's language is not Japanese. You can change it in "言語 / Language" in the Settings tab.

4Load the room ID

On the phone, press "Enter room ID", choose "📷 Scan QR with camera", and point the camera at the room ID QR code from step 2. Once it is read, the connection starts automatically.

If the camera cannot be used, type the ID shown in the console into the box and press "OK".
The first time a device connects, a dialog asking whether to allow the connection appears on the PC. Nothing happens until someone presses "Yes".

5Start streaming

Once connected, press the Start button. If the PC's screen appears on the phone, you are done 🎉

To hear sound, press the audio button so it shows "Audio ON". The button shows the current state. It starts muted, so it first shows "Audio OFF".

If the video stutters, lower the bitrate (quality) number and press Start again.

＋Control it with the mouse

The pad at the bottom of the screen works right away. Slide to move the pointer, tap to left-click, and long-press to right-click. To type text, switch the "Input" drop-down to text input.

When you control from a computer, nothing needs switching: just move the mouse over the video.

To show the screen you are receiving to other people with comments, use the Restream tab (see the User Guide for details).
